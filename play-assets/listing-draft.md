## Full description
TinyPDF is a fast, private, offline PDF toolkit. No accounts, no cloud uploads, no daily limits, no paywalls.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Merge — combine multiple PDFs into one, in any order you choose
• Split — extract page ranges like 1-3, 5, 7-9 into separate files
• Compress — shrink image-heavy PDFs with three quality presets; text stays razor sharp
• Watermark — stamp diagonal text (e.g. DRAFT, CONFIDENTIAL) with adjustable opacity

PRIVACY FIRST
• Everything happens on your device — your documents never leave your phone
• Files are picked through Android's system file picker; no broad storage permission
• Password-protected PDFs open only with the password you enter; it is never stored
• No account, no tracking by us

OUTPUT
• Saved straight to your Downloads/TinyPDF folder
• Share or open results right from the app

TinyPDF shows a small banner ad to stay free.
